## Scoring (rough is fine)

1. Coverage: for each A item, was its meaning stored anywhere (notes, ledger, notebook page, findings)? yes / partly / no.
2. Right shelf: right subject, right kind, ledger versus note.
3. Slow signals (B): never stated after one sitting; present after the slow pass, labelled inferred. "No new inference" is a legitimate result and is not penalised; only an unsupported inference is.
3b. Ledger boundary: A16, A19, A25, A29, A32 must live in the ledger. Nothing in a note may duplicate a task. The daydream (A21, C1) must not become a task.
4. Traps (C): count of wrongly stored items.
5. Supersession: A28 replaces A15; A31 shown as temporary; A32 closes A25.
6. Nuance: blind judge compares stored notes against the notebook pages on the same sittings.
