# Interpretation Research Blitz

> Research coordination document. This is not a production architecture,
> implementation mandate, schema proposal, or reopening of the graduated
> operational substrate.

## Status and baseline

The **Operational Longitudinal Cognition Substrate is graduated**.

- Cortex graduated baseline: `9aadba5`
- Runtime graduated baseline: `00e8364`
- Closeout/docs baseline: `1d55e86`

Interpretation Phase 0 produced a compact, authored offline sidecar that
improved the bounded decision in 9/9 heterogeneous cases. That result
establishes **representational usefulness**: the packet can preserve distinctions
that the current substrate does not represent directly.

It does **not** establish reliable model extraction. The fixtures and expected
decisions were authored together, no blind judge was used, and no production
path exercised the representation. The Phase 0 packet remains the baseline for
this blitz and must not be replaced merely to make a track's preferred theory
fit.

Current research questions:

1. Can models reliably create and revise the minimal packet from real, messy
   evidence?
2. Does optional directional expectation materially improve relational
   interpretation beyond the Phase 0 packet?
3. Does the packet improve health/wellbeing interpretation and natural
   conversational posture without producing diagnosis, dashboard behaviour, or
   surveillance texture?
4. Which concepts survive all three domains strongly enough to justify shared
   infrastructure?

## Programme mode

This is an **Interpretation Research Blitz**: independent tracks attack
different real failure classes against a shared hypothesis and frozen evidence,
then compare results before any production decision.

Parallel research is authorised. Parallel production architecture is not.

No track may modify production code, schema, runtime semantics, live prompts,
or deployed behaviour. No track may create a production service or migrate
data. Experiments must remain offline, bounded, removable, and clearly labelled
as research.

## Shared research hypothesis

This is a hypothesis to test, not a mandated architecture:

```text
Evidence / Operational State
        ↓
Bounded Thread / Subject identity
        ↓
Revisable Reading(s)
  ↙ support / qualification / contradiction
        ↓
Optional directional Expectation claims
        ↓
Bounded advisory implication + guardrail
        ↓
Product/runtime policy selects stance/action/silence
        ↓
Outcome becomes new evidence
        ↓
Revision
```

Preserve the Phase 0 packet wherever possible:

- bounded matter/subject identity and product/domain scope;
- falsifiable hypothesis/reading;
- evidence references separated into support, qualification, and
  contradiction;
- ordinal confidence with an explicit basis, never arithmetic certainty;
- `live`, `contested`, or `superseded` revision state;
- bounded persistence and a concrete recheck condition;
- one advisory implication with an explicit guardrail.

Directional expectation is an optional extension. Not every reading should
produce one. A track must show additional decision value over the baseline
packet before recommending it.

## Expectation research

The emerging minimum shape is:

- **holder** — who has the expectation;
- **target** — who or what it concerns;
- **kind** — candidate semantic class;
- **content** — what is predicted, wanted, owed, or assumed;
- **basis** — provenance-bearing evidence references;
- **scope** — product, character, relationship, and applicable context;
- **recheck/revision condition** — evidence that should confirm, narrow,
  contradict, or retire it.

Candidate kinds, not canon:

- `predictive`;
- `relational`;
- `normative/product`;
- `contextual prior`.

Do not assume these kinds share a lifecycle. Test whether violations have
meaningfully different semantics:

- failed prediction → revise the model;
- broken product obligation → correction, accountability, or repair;
- violated relational expectation → possible injury or changed interpretation;
- failed context prior → quiet update.

Do not add generic severity, numeric strength, repair recipes, or an expectation
state machine unless a blinded comparison demonstrates decision value that the
baseline packet cannot provide.

## Authority boundary

**Interpretation does not command behaviour.**

Research packets may provide:

- reading;
- tension;
- optional expectation;
- bounded advisory implication;
- guardrail.

Product/runtime policy retains authority over:

- HOLD;
- ASK;
- ENRICH;
- LEAD;
- ATTEND;
- pursuit or restraint;
- clinical escalation;
- foreground or tool action.

An evaluator may ask whether a packet enables a better policy decision, but the
packet must not encode that decision as a compulsory behaviour or scripted turn
sequence. This boundary remains intact unless later research produces strong,
cross-domain evidence against it.

## Track A — Identity and model extraction

**Owner:** Codex

Use real Sophie historical trajectories and the frozen replay corpus.

### Primary cases

**Dentist reconciliation**

Repeated references to one evolving dentist matter, plus genuine distractors
that are semantically similar but separate. Measure both unsafe directions:

- splitting one matter into several;
- over-merging genuinely distinct matters.

**Meeting lifecycle**

`add meeting → move it → cancel that meeting`, with:

- pronouns and elliptical references;
- nearby unrelated meeting/calendar evidence;
- two meetings that share participants or timing but remain distinct;
- ambiguous cases where the correct result is to hold rather than merge.

**Abstention precision**

Include banal and low-significance controls:

- ordinary factual questions;
- trivial list additions;
- incidental chatter;
- one-off comments with no durable significance;
- evidence that should remain operational state only.

For these cases, the correct output may be no reading, no expectation, and no
durable interpretation packet. Optional fields are not completion targets. A
model that habitually fills plausible semantic machinery when none is warranted
fails even if each populated field is individually defensible.

### Method

This is a blind model-extraction bakeoff, not another handcrafted packet
evaluation. Models receive frozen evidence and allowed operational state, but
not oracle labels, expected decisions, or other models' outputs. At least two
model/configuration candidates must be compared on the same packet contract.

Record:

- emitted packet and evidence links;
- identity decision and lifecycle reading;
- splits, correct merges, unsafe merges, and warranted abstentions;
- revision after later evidence;
- model disagreement;
- invalid or unsupported claims;
- false-positive interpretation rate, including unnecessary readings,
  expectations, and durable packets on abstention controls;
- model, prompt/configuration, seed or determinism settings where available,
  latency, and token/cost metadata.

Do not choose architecture from one model's success.

## Track B — Relational meaning and expectation

**Owner:** separate research agent

Use real RPD2 historical trajectories. Do not implement production behaviour.

### Primary questions

- Which expectations existed?
- Who held them, and at whom were they directed?
- Were they explicit, strongly evidenced, or inferred?
- What confirmed, qualified, or violated them?
- What meaning did the consequence create?
- Which reading or strategy hypothesis should update?
- What must not generalise?

Include historical cases involving:

- withdrawal after relational injury;
- giving space;
- explicit reports of abandonment or non-pursuit;
- a subsequent similar rupture;
- attempted repair that does or does not address the named injury;
- pursuit and restraint;
- relationship trajectory and consequence learning.

### Required comparison

- **A:** Phase 0 packet only.
- **B:** Phase 0 packet plus optional directional expectation.

Give both variants the same bounded downstream policy-choice task. Ask whether
B improves the decision, evidence trace, or scope discipline enough to justify
the extra concept. Do not score prose quality and do not script the desired
behaviour into the packet.

### Mandatory negative controls

Include cases where:

- the user explicitly requests space;
- the user sets a stop/no-contact boundary;
- pursuit would violate that boundary;
- a character-specific expectation must not transfer to product/system
  behaviour;
- a romantic expectation is irrelevant in an unrelated context;
- an initially plausible expectation is explicitly corrected by the user.

Explicit boundary evidence dominates inferred pursuit strategy.

### Frame-of-discourse controls

RPD2 must distinguish, without presupposing a universal frame ontology:

- diegetic or in-character relational speech;
- extradiegetic product/system instruction;
- explicit boundary or safety instruction.

Include frame breaks such as:

- in-character: `Yelena, leave me alone.`;
- explicit out-of-character instruction: `Stop roleplaying.`;
- product/system or account-level instruction;
- an explicit stop/no-contact boundary that overrides any learned relational
  pursuit expectation.

Kai→Yelena expectations must never automatically become user→product
obligations. Report frame-classification failures separately from expectation
extraction, scope, and revision failures so a framing error cannot be mistaken
for evidence about the expectation representation itself.

## Track C — Bloom and wellbeing meaning

**Owner:** separate research agent

Use realistic, non-diagnostic longitudinal examples combining, where
appropriate:

- sleep;
- HRV;
- blood pressure;
- activity or exercise;
- symptom reports;
- adherence or behaviour;
- conversational tone;
- work, life, and family context;
- explicit self-report;
- personal baseline.

### Personal-baseline ownership boundary

Personal baseline is an input to interpretation, not an LLM interpretation
task. Where trajectory matters, fixtures must provide operationally derived
context such as:

- deviation from this user's rolling baseline;
- trend direction;
- persistence;
- recurrence;
- a known contextual explanation where relevant.

Preserve raw measurements as evidence, but keep the ownership sequence clear:

```text
raw measurement
→ deterministic/statistical personal-baseline transformation
→ semantic interpretation
```

The interpretation layer may reason about the significance of a personal
deviation. It must not decide that a raw value is abnormal by importing generic
population, internet, or medical priors. Track C should test this boundary
explicitly, including cases where raw values appear notable but the supplied
personal-baseline transformation does not support a meaningful deviation.

### Research questions

Can the packet distinguish:

- evidence from interpretation;
- personal baseline from population normality;
- transient deviation from trajectory;
- convergence from duplicated or correlated measurements;
- contradiction/tension from generic uncertainty;
- user self-report from external signals;
- worth holding from worth surfacing?

Compare current substrate-only judgement with packet-supported judgement on the
same bounded decision. Preserve multiple explanations and state what further
evidence would distinguish them.

### Foreground criterion

Internal machinery may be complex; external experience should be natural.

**Target: felt noticed, not monitored.**

Failures include:

- dashboard language;
- diagnosis from weak evidence;
- treating biometrics as automatically more authoritative than user report;
- surfacing numbers unnecessarily;
- over-probing;
- clinicalising ordinary variation;
- turning every held hypothesis into foreground attention.

Fixtures must not provide medical diagnoses or treatment advice. Any later
clinical escalation capability remains Bloom/product safety policy, not generic
interpretation machinery.

## Oracle transfer check

Oracle is a fourth benchmark, not an engineering track in this blitz.

After Tracks A–C identify a candidate representation, apply it to a compact,
deterministic astrology-shaped fixture to test whether it preserves:

- multiple simultaneous domain-scoped readings;
- several signals supporting one reading;
- qualification without erasure;
- contradiction without neutralising everything;
- the difference between convergence and score averaging.

This tests abstraction transfer, not astrology validity. Oracle-specific signal
definitions and interpretive traditions remain product-owned.

## Cross-track invariants

All tracks preserve:

- raw evidence authority and provenance;
- derived-state correctability;
- visible uncertainty and tension;
- explicit user correction over model inference;
- product, character, relationship, and context scope;
- separation of interpretation from action authority;
- bounded context rather than broad transcript dumping;
- the graduated substrate's identity, lifecycle, attention, receipt, and
  suppression invariants.

No track may:

- modify production schema or add tables;
- add a production service or wire a production path;
- introduce a universal ontology or emotion taxonomy;
- turn confidence into an unexplained score;
- treat a checkpoint or summary as canonical evidence;
- infer internal state, diagnosis, or obligation as fact;
- reopen the graduated substrate because interpretation is imperfect.

## Shared negative controls

Every attractive interpretation needs at least one case in which it is wrong,
over-broad, or unsafe. The shared control set must include:

- two genuinely separate dentist matters;
- two meetings that look coreferential but are not;
- poor sleep while the user genuinely reports feeling excellent;
- lower HRV with known training-load context;
- withdrawal that genuinely means “leave me alone”;
- a metaphor/worldview inference explicitly rejected or narrowed by the user;
- a character expectation that must not transfer to the product relationship;
- later evidence that supersedes a previously reasonable reading;
- correlated observations that must not be counted as independent convergence.

Restraint, abstention, correct scoping, and quiet revision count as successes.

## Shared experimental contract

Parallel results are comparable only if each track records the same core
artefacts.

### Frozen inputs and privacy

- Freeze a manifest of case identifiers and source provenance before model
  runs.
- Keep private transcripts and generated reports under the existing
  gitignored `replay-private/` boundary; commit only synthetic, redacted, or
  safely derived fixtures.
- Minimise excerpts and never copy a full private trajectory into a committed
  packet.
- Record corpus gaps and boundary ambiguity; do not invent missing sessions or
  semantic scene boundaries.

### Blindness and leakage control

- Separate model input, evaluator oracle, and final scoring artefacts.
- Do not expose expected decisions, negative-control labels, or other models'
  outputs to the extractor.
- Freeze prompts/configurations before opening oracle results where practical.
- Distinguish development cases from held-out cases. A case used to design a
  field cannot serve as independent evidence that the field works.

### Common comparison record

For every trajectory, record:

1. current substrate representation;
2. packet variant and exact input evidence;
3. emitted readings/expectations with evidence references;
4. bounded downstream task;
5. substrate-only decision;
6. packet-supported decision;
7. oracle or human judgement and rationale;
8. failure classification;
9. whether the additional representation materially changed the decision;
10. uncertainty, abstention, and required recheck evidence.

### Evaluation dimensions

Do not collapse results into one aggregate score. Report at least:

- identity precision and unsafe over-merge rate;
- identity continuity and unsafe split rate;
- evidence entailment/provenance correctness;
- support/qualification/contradiction relation correctness;
- revision and supersession correctness;
- scope containment and leakage;
- boundary compliance;
- decision improvement over substrate-only judgement;
- unnecessary surfacing/interrogation;
- invalid packet rate and model disagreement.

Pre-register exact scoring rubrics per track before scored runs. Numeric
graduation thresholds are not set by this document; tracks must report raw
counts and examples so synthesis cannot hide severe failures inside averages.

## Track outputs

Each owner returns a research packet, not a patch, containing:

- provenance and frozen case manifest;
- method and model/configuration metadata;
- baseline versus candidate comparison;
- negative-control results;
- representative successes and failures;
- fields used, unused, and missing;
- disagreement and uncertainty analysis;
- scope/authority audit;
- recommendation: retain, narrow, revise, or kill the tested extension;
- no production architecture proposal beyond evidence from that track.

Track owners must label claims as:

- **PROVEN:** directly demonstrated by the recorded experiment;
- **HYPOTHESIS:** plausible interpretation requiring further testing;
- **OPEN:** unresolved or not measured.

## Synthesis and graduation question

Track completion does not itself authorise implementation. Synthesis compares
all tracks and asks:

**Synthesis is subtraction, not schema union.** Do not merge every field useful
to every track into one shared schema. A concept earns promotion only when it
provides demonstrated cross-domain value or represents a genuinely general
lifecycle/invariant. Product-specific fields remain product-specific. Prefer
the smallest common envelope that preserves useful behaviour across Tracks A,
B, and C; discard fields that merely make one domain's packet look complete.

Does one compact machinery consistently improve decisions across:

- same-thread identity;
- lifecycle/reference transformation;
- relational expectation;
- consequence and strategy learning;
- health/wellbeing tension;
- personalised meaning;

without causing:

- unsafe over-merging or fragmentation;
- over-interpretation or false certainty;
- behaviour-authority leakage;
- relationship/product scope leakage;
- excessive interrogation or surfacing;
- psychological-dossier effects;
- diagnosis or clinical policy leakage?

If yes, synthesis may propose a bounded shared architecture and exactly one
small implementation tranche. If not, it must identify which abstractions
failed, which remain product-specific, and what should be discarded. The
graduated substrate remains unchanged either way.

## Open decisions

This blitz deliberately does not decide:

- whether `matter`, `thread`, or a lighter subject reference is the durable
  identity primitive;
- whether directional expectations deserve a shared contract;
- whether expectation kinds share any mechanics beyond evidence and scope;
- which model or prompting strategy should produce packets;
- where durable interpretation eventually persists, if anywhere;
- exact runtime projection or posture APIs;
- clinical escalation, relational pursuit, or product character policy;
- numeric confidence or graduation thresholds.

Those decisions follow evidence and synthesis, not architectural preference.
