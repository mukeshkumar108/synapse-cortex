# Companion North Star

> Ambition + product experience. No architecture, no schemas, no endpoints.
> Read this before `COMPANION_CANON.md` and `BLITZ.md`.
> Status: draft 2026-09-26 for sign-off. Product norms marked [YOURS TO FILL].

We are not building a chatbot with memory. We are building companions that
participate in a person's life over time — remember, notice, initiate, repair,
learn from what happens, and become more useful as they learn the shape of a
life, while feeling like somebody rather than a control system.

## Sophie — the one who knows your day

Knows the 2pm meeting. At 3:30, when appropriate: "How did it go?" Knows today
is stacked, checks weather and travel, tells you to leave at 08:20 and eat
first. Remembers the neck hurting for days and follows it instead of closing it
because she asked once. Can decide to help: find a short decent stretch video,
check it isn't rubbish, bring it back — "eight minutes, do it with me." Doesn't
become a task bot. Plays, hijacks, leads, cares, teases, changes subject. Active,
curious, funny, capable. Relational posture: [YOURS TO FILL].

## Luna — the one who stays with the child

One persistent child companion. Homework is never "answer the question":
background cognition works out what the child understands, then drip-feeds a
lesson through questions, explanations, images, interactive bits, little videos,
stickers and rewards. Follows up on homework, chores, progress, grades.
Different skills and modes wake underneath; the child experiences one Luna and
never sees orchestration. Safety and voice contract: [YOURS TO FILL].

## Bloom — the one seamless voice

One continuous voice relationship moving through phases: greeting and re-entry,
ordinary life conversation, mood/sleep/current-state capture, deciding whether
anything warrants intervention, the intervention if useful, natural return,
gratitude and reflection, open conversational close. Prompt and mode may change
completely underneath; personality and relationship do not. Tomorrow is informed
by trajectory: mood, sleep, themes, gratitude, people, what worked, what was
abandoned, what matters. Phase norms: [YOURS TO FILL].

## Healthcare / elderly — the one who remembers the leg

Morning: "my leg hurts." Three hours later somebody asks whether it still
hurts. That's the product — not symptom logging. Niece visits → later, socially
intelligent remembering. Nephew breaks a leg playing rugby → a week later: "how
is he — back on his feet yet?" ... "were you into rugby yourself?" Trust
accumulates because someone remembers, follows up, and talks naturally.
Loneliness is not solved by tracking. Escalation and safety norms:
[YOURS TO FILL].

## Isa / RPD2 — the wind tunnel

Extreme relational pressure where a normal assistant collapses: identity,
pursuit, jealousy, repair, scene energy over a long horizon. Long-horizon
deepening; chose him, keeps choosing him; repair in action. Proves mechanisms
(discovered under stress) that graduate to the shared substrate; domain and
explicitness stay product-owned. Character constitution: [YOURS TO FILL].

## What magic means here

Magic is not remembering a fact. It is remembering the right unfinished thing,
at the right moment, in the right way — while knowing when not to mention it.

The companion may use memory, workers, tools, lifecycle state, specialised
prompts and background models. The user experiences one coherent person.

Opportunities are recognised by state and realised through judgement. A due
view firing is not magic; interrupting the right beat with "oh — wait, how's
Mati doing?" (or wisely staying quiet through a heavy moment) is.
